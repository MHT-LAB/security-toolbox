# MS-ISAC Real-Time Indicator Feeds

Free automated malicious-IP/domain indicator feed for US state, local, tribal, and territorial (SLTT) government members via the Center for Internet Security.

**Links:** [Official site](https://www.cisecurity.org/ms-isac/services/real-time-indicator-feeds)

## Overview

MS-ISAC is CIS's ISAC specifically for SLTT government entities, and its Real-Time Indicator Feeds provide automated blocklist-style indicators to eligible members — a free, government-focused parallel to community feeds like abuse.ch, but membership-gated rather than open to the general public.

## Install / Deploy

```text
Requires MS-ISAC membership (free for eligible SLTT government organizations) —
apply at cisecurity.org/ms-isac; feed delivery/integration details are provided
to members directly upon enrollment.
```

## Common Commands

Not applicable — delivery mechanism and integration details are provided upon membership approval.

## Lab Exercise

If your organization qualifies for MS-ISAC membership, pipe this feed into your firewall/DNS blocklist or SIEM the same way you would [AIS](cisa-ais.md) or [OTX](otx.md) above — it's free but membership-gated, not open to the public.
