# National Council of ISACs

Directory of Information Sharing and Analysis Centers by sector — membership is usually free, join the one for your industry (financial services, healthcare, etc.).

**Links:** [Official site](https://www.nationalisacs.org)

## Overview

Each ISAC serves one sector (financial services, healthcare, energy, and more) and shares sector-specific threat advisories, incident data, and best practices among its members — the National Council's directory is how you find which one applies to your organization.

## Install / Deploy

```text
Browse the directory at nationalisacs.org to identify your sector's ISAC,
then apply for membership directly through that ISAC's own site (terms/cost vary by ISAC).
```

## Common Commands

Not applicable — a membership organization, not a tool.

## Lab Exercise

Find your organization's (or your employer's) applicable ISAC and get an analyst or IT lead onto its mailing list/portal — most of the value here is the sector-specific advisory feed, not the directory page itself.
