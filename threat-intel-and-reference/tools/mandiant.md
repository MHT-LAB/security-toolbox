# Mandiant (Google Cloud)

Commercial threat-intel research and incident-response consulting — Google Cloud's incident-response and intelligence arm, widely cited for breach investigations and APT research.

**Links:** [Official site](https://cloud.google.com/security/mandiant) — commercial product/consulting, no public repo.

## Overview

Mandiant is best known for its incident-response consulting and the annual M-Trends report — a widely-cited, freely-readable summary of real breach investigation trends worth reading even without engaging their consulting services. Several free tools elsewhere in this repo (FLARE-VM, FLOSS) originate from Mandiant's FLARE team.

## Install / Deploy

```text
Commercial consulting/subscription through cloud.google.com/security/mandiant;
the free annual M-Trends report is available directly from their site.
```
