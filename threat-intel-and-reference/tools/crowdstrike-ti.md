# CrowdStrike Threat Intelligence

Commercial threat-intel and adversary-tracking service — named adversary tracking (e.g. "FANCY BEAR"-style designations) and deep attribution research.

**Links:** [Official site](https://www.crowdstrike.com/platform/threat-intelligence/) — commercial product, no public repo.

## Overview

CrowdStrike's intel service is best known for its adversary-naming taxonomy and attribution depth, typically consumed through their Falcon platform — worth knowing about for context even without a subscription, since their public blog/reports are widely referenced industry-wide.

## Install / Deploy

```text
Commercial subscription through CrowdStrike; public threat reports and blog
posts are readable without a subscription at crowdstrike.com/blog.
```

## Common Commands

Not applicable — commercial platform, accessed via its own Falcon console/API.
