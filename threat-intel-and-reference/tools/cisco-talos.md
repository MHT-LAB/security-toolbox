# Cisco Talos Intelligence Group

Free threat-intel research, IP/domain reputation lookups, and vulnerability disclosures — one of the largest commercial threat-research teams publishing freely.

**Links:** [Official site](https://talosintelligence.com)

## Overview

Talos publishes detailed malware/campaign research and runs a free reputation-lookup service for IPs/domains (used behind the scenes by many Cisco security products) — a solid free source for both deep-dive reading and quick reputation checks.

## Install / Deploy

```text
No installation — browse talosintelligence.com, or use its reputation
lookup directly at talosintelligence.com/reputation_center.
```

## Common Commands

Not applicable — web-based reputation lookup and blog/research portal.

## Lab Exercise

Check an IP address from your own firewall/Suricata logs against Talos's reputation center, alongside [AbuseIPDB](../../recon-and-osint/tools/abuseipdb.md), to compare the two sources' verdicts.
